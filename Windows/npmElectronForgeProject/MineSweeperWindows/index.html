🎯 OBJECTIVE

Uncover all non-mine tiles on the grid without detonating a single landmine!

🔢 NUMBERS EXPLAINED

Revealed numbers (1–8) indicate exactly how many hidden mines are adjacent to that tile (including diagonals).

📱 TOUCH CONTROLS

  • Tap Tile: Reveals (digs) a tile in ⛏️ DIG Mode or flags a tile in 🚩 FLAG Mode.
  • Mode Toggle: Switch between ⛏️ DIG and 🚩 FLAG buttons at the bottom.
  • Long Press / Hold: Quick-flags a tile in DIG mode.
  • Chording / Quick-Clear: Tap a revealed number tile whose adjacent flag count matches its number to safely uncover surrounding unflagged tiles!

💾 STREAK & SAVING

  • Winning a level increments your active win streak. Tap NEXT ➔ to carry your streak into the next minefield.
  • The game continuously saves your active board state and high scores in LocalStorage so you can close and resume anytime!
